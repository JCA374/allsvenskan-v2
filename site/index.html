How it works

Everything on this page is calculated in your browser. It loads every Allsvenskan result since 2012 (from football-data.co.uk) and the remaining fixtures, then fits a Dixon-Coles model. Each team gets an attack and a defence rating, and recent matches count more than old ones. The page then plays out the rest of the season thousands of times. Ties are separated by goal difference, then goals scored.
